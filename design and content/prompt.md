# Image Generation Prompts for Art Courses Platform

**IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AI GENERATOR:**
Copy and paste this base style prompt and negative prompt along with each individual scene below to ensure the AI generates images specifically for an **Online Course Platform** (like MasterClass or Domestika), rather than generic stock photos.

> **Base Style Prompt (Append to every prompt):**
> Premium online course promotional photography, MasterClass aesthetic, e-learning platform thumbnail, highly detailed, cinematic lighting, focused educational environment, aspirational learning.
> 
> **Negative Prompt (Use to prevent bad generations):**
> text, words, typography, logo, watermark, cartoon, anime, messy, low resolution, generic stock photo look, poorly lit.

**Global Guidelines:**
*   **Aspect Ratio:** Horizontal (16:9) - --ar 16:9
*   **Models:** Whenever human subjects are featured, they **MUST be South East Asian** to reflect the platform's cultural focus.
*   **Educational Context:** Every image must include subtle cues of online learning, teaching, or studying (e.g., laptops, digital tablets, stylus pens, notebooks, professional camera recording rigs, or a clear instructor-student dynamic).
*   **Variety:** Every image uses a distinct camera angle and framing.
